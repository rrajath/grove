When two devices edit the same `.org` file while disconnected, Syncthing resolves the collision by keeping both copies: your device's version, plus a `.sync-conflict-*` file holding the other side's changes. Grove detects these copies during its normal sync pass and surfaces them for resolution rather than silently picking one.

## The conflict screen

Opening a flagged notebook (or choosing **Resolve conflict** from its long-press menu on the Notebooks screen) shows a warning banner and a unified diff between your current file and the conflict copy.

Three choices:

- **Keep current**: discard the conflict copy, keep what's on your device
- **Keep conflict copy**: replace your device's version with the conflict copy's content
- **Keep both**: the current copy and the conflict copy get interspersed so that nothing is lost

There's no automatic merge in v1; every conflict gets a deliberate choice. There's no hunk-level conflict resolution either.
