---
title: Undo
description: A consistent undo snackbar behind nearly every mutating action in Grove, so quick gestures stay safe to use.
---

Because so much of Grove's interaction model is built around fast, low-friction gestures — swipe-to-done, swipe-to-schedule, one-tap "Move to today" — almost every mutating action shows an undo snackbar (or toast, for simpler cases) immediately afterward.

## What's covered

State changes, scheduling changes, archiving, refiling, and bulk actions like Agenda's "Move to today" all snapshot the affected file(s) before mutating them. If you tap **Undo** in the snackbar, Grove restores the pre-mutation content exactly.

For actions that touch multiple files at once (like a bulk overdue reschedule), the snapshot covers every affected file, so a single Undo tap reverts the whole batch, not just one item.

## Why this matters for the gesture-heavy screens

Swipe actions in [Outline View](/features/outline-view), [Agenda](/features/agenda), and [Search](/features/search) are intentionally fast — a single swipe commits immediately rather than asking for confirmation first. Undo is what makes that safe: you get a brief window to reverse a swipe that fired on the wrong row or with the wrong intent, without every gesture needing a confirmation dialog that would slow the whole app down.
