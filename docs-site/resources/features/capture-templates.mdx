Capture Templates define what shows up in the [Capture](/features/capture) picker. Manage them from Settings → Capture Templates, or via the **Manage** link inside the picker itself.

## Creating and editing a template

Each template has:

- **Name** and **icon** — shown as the tile in the Capture picker
- **Target file** — which notebook the entry gets written to
- **Insert location**:
  - **Top of file** — prepended right after any file-level preface
  - **Bottom of file** — appended at the end
  - **Under heading** — inserted as a child of a specific heading, matched either by exact heading name or by `CUSTOM_ID` (CUSTOM_ID is the recommended match target, since it survives the heading being renamed)
  - **Datetree, by date or by date+time** — auto-creates a Year → Month → Day (→ Time) chain of headings if it doesn't already exist, then inserts under today's leaf
- **Template body** — the org text to insert, including any [placeholder](/features/placeholders) tokens

Templates are reorderable — drag to change the order they appear in the Capture picker.

## Notification toggle

A separate toggle on this screen turns on an **ongoing capture notification**: a persistent, low-priority notification that opens the Capture picker in one tap, for quick access without unlocking into the app first.

## Shortcut sync

Configured templates automatically stay in sync with Android's long-press launcher shortcuts on the Grove app icon — add or remove a template and the shortcut list updates to match, no separate configuration needed.
