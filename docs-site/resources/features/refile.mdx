---
title: Refile
description: Move a heading and its subtree to a different notebook or location without cutting and pasting raw text.
---

Refile moves a heading — and everything nested under it — to a new home: a different position in the same file, a different notebook, or your configured auto-archive location.

## Where it's used

Refile is the same drill-down picker reused in three places:

- The **Refile** swipe action in [Outline View](/features/outline-view)
- The manual **Archive** action on a heading
- Auto-archive's destination resolution, when the "Auto-archive done items" setting is enabled (see [Notes Behavior](/features/notes-behavior))

## How it works

The picker drills down: pick a notebook, then optionally drill into a specific heading to refile *under*. Confirming moves the subtree's full org text — heading, properties, tags, body, and any nested children — out of its current position and appends it at the chosen destination.

Like other mutating actions, a Refile is reversible via the [undo snackbar](/features/undo) immediately after.
